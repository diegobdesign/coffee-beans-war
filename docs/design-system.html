Design system · v0.1 · The Grounds

Coffee Beans War

A saturated low-poly diorama at first light, flat-shaded, no outlines, one warm key light, milk-white sky, a black lake of coffee steaming in the lowland. Tone: deadpan. Three decisions to react to: deadpan with no exclamation marks anywhere in the game; the world is green, red and violet-grey and the beans are the only brown; the UI is thermal receipt paper and stamped bag labels. Everything below is rendered with the real tokens, not described.

02 · The world

One composition, learned once

World camera: perspective, 30° FOV, pitch 52°, yaw fixed at 35°. Ridge top-right, Belt on the diagonal, Brew bottom-left, The Roaster at the centre. No rotation, ever.

05 · The machines

Coffee kit, ink enamel, accent trim

Moka pot mortar
Default: Robusta · high lob

Default: Liberica · plunger charge
Espresso machine
Default: Arabica · gauge is the charge UI
Aeropress sniper
Unlock at Medium roast · ignores 50% steam

All four are primitives, at most 400 triangles, one shared Lambert material plus one glass. The barrel pivots to the aim angle, so the machine is the aim indicator.

06 · The ammo

Beans, fired

Green bean ∞
Five dots fading over 400ms. Impact: six green chips and a click.
Dark roast ×3
A single smoke thread. Drops sooner. Impact: ring of 12 chips, a low thud.
Ground coffee ×2
Six pellets fanned ±6°, the only cubes in the game. No trail. Grinder burst on fire.
Full cup ×1
Tumbles end over end with a steam spiral. Impact: 2-unit splash decal, 16 droplets, crema ring.
